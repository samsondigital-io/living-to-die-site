About the Book

A Mother's Fight Against Medical Negligence

When Diane Melton was diagnosed with breast cancer, she entrusted her life to the medical professionals sworn to protect it. What followed was a harrowing journey through misdiagnosis, institutional failures, and a healthcare system that too often prioritizes process over patients.

Living To Die is more than a memoir. It is a testament to the resilience of the human spirit and a clarion call for accountability in American healthcare. Through intimate prose and unflinching honesty, Diane shares the story that demanded to be told.

Key Themes

  • Medical negligence & patient advocacy
  • The fight for justice in healthcare
  • Family, resilience & hope
  • Navigating the legal system
  • Finding strength through adversity

About the Author

Diane Melton

Diane Melton is a mother, advocate, and survivor whose personal experience with medical negligence ignited a passion for patient rights and systemic accountability.

Her story has resonated with healthcare professionals, legal scholars, and families navigating the same institutional barriers she once faced. Through speaking engagements and her writing, Diane continues to fight for the changes she believes every patient deserves.

Living To Die is her first book — and the culmination of a decade-long journey from patient to advocate.

— Diane Melton
